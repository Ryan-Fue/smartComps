About smartComps

smartComps is a valuation engine that uses Machine Learning to estimate company valuations. It uniquely synthesizes hard financial multiples (Ex. Revenue, EBITDA) with high-dimensional NLP (Natural Language Processing) to "understand" the qualitative nature of a business through its business summary.

How to Use:
  1. Engine Setup: Open the "Engine Setup" accordion. Select the financial features you want the model to learn from and choose your target variable. Click "Initialize & Train" to build a custom model optimized for your selection (This may take a while).
  2. Input Metrics: Once the engine is ready, the interface will generate fields for your selected features. Fill in the financial data for the company you wish to value.
  3. Qualitative Context: Paste the company's business summary or a strategic overview into the text area. The AI uses this to identify sectoral peers and qualitative similarities in its training set.
  4. Generate Report: Click "Generate Valuation Report" to receive a predicted valuation and a confidence range derived from the model's historical performance (MDAPE).
